SPiDeR-FM: Risk-Controlled Continual Test-Time Adaptation for Semantic Segmentation with Foundation Models
Kranthi Kumar Sariputi
British Machine Vision Conference (BMVC 2026)   (Under Review)

Introduces SPiDeR-FM, a continual TTA framework that reframes online adaptation as risk-controlled learning using CUSUM-based shift detection, conformal pseudo-label filtering, and segmentation-aware masked reconstruction with frozen DINOv2 + LoRA adapters. Achieves verifiable adaptation risk ρ̂ₜ ≤ α = 0.10 throughout deployment on the ACDC benchmark.

Supervisor: Prof. Balasubramanian Raman, Head of Department, CSE, IIT Roorkee
